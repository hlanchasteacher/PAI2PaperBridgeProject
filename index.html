Structural Engineering Challenge

Welcome, engineers!

In universities and engineering schools around the world, students face challenges with limited resources. This forces them to develop their problem-solving skills and understand how physics work.

Your new mission is to span a gap by building a bridge.

⚠️ The Catch:

You cannot use rigid materials like wood, plastic, or metal. You can only use paper. You will have to rely on geometry and design to make it strong!

👥

Form Your Engineering Firm

Great structures are built by great teams.

For this project, you can choose how you want to work:

  • Individually (Solo)
  • In pairs (2 people)
  • In a group of maximum 3 people
✍️

Action Required Right Now:

Take a piece of paper. Write down the total number of members in your group, and write the full names of everyone in it. You will need this for your final report!

🔬

The Material Lab

Before building, you must test the materials (printer paper, aluminum foil, toilet paper, etc.). Write down every weight you test and note exactly when the paper breaks to find your maximum viable weight.

1. Tension (Pulling)

Tape a strip of paper to the table, hang a cup from it, and add weights one by one. How much weight can it hold before tearing?

2. Compression (Squashing)

Roll the paper into a cylinder. Place books on top one by one. How many books can it support before collapsing?

3. Folding & Adhesion

Fold the paper to see if it keeps its shape. Glue two pieces together. Does the glue work? Does it stay folded?
